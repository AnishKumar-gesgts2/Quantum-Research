# GBS Tutor Guide

Use [[Learning Path]] and [[Learning Progress]]. The aim is for Anish to draft and revise the proposal independently, not merely repeat a polished abstract.

## Teaching workflow

1. Read the selected lesson and its prerequisites. Ask for a short explanation of the relevant prerequisite before deciding the pace.
2. Start with the physical or probability intuition, define symbols, and work one small example.
3. Ask one question at a time. Let Anish attempt the calculation or explanation before revealing an answer. Offer a hint when requested.
4. When an answer is wrong, identify the exact step and use a new example to check the repair.
5. End with the lesson's proposal task. Ask Anish to draft the paragraph and explain its factual support before suggesting wording changes.
6. Record demonstrated understanding and unresolved questions when asked to update the progress note. Do not infer mastery from reading.

## Concepts to keep separate

- Quadrature covariance and binary click correlations.
- Optical inputs, output modes, photons, clicks, and physical detectors.
- Exact probability evaluation, observable estimation, and valid detector sampling.
- Agreement with a calibrated model and agreement with hardware records.
- Small full-distribution TVD and large-scale selected diagnostics.
- The earlier selective-correlation prototype and the active phase-space reproduction.
- Improved fit error and improved measured computational cost.
- A paper's prediction, the project's hypothesis, a provisional budget, and a measured result.

## Evidence rules for teaching

Consult [[Sources and Project Map]] for authoritative local notes and papers. All toy distributions and resource examples in the lessons are illustrative. Do not describe them as physical experimental results.

The skeptical audit controls the interpretation of prior performance. It supports numerical checks and an information-selection effect while rejecting the tested enumerative architecture as a computational improvement. It reused the earlier held-out cases; it is not a new held-out confirmation.

For method details, read the actual source equations and implementation. Do not fill a missing artifact or convention with an unlabeled guess. Metric-specific significance rules need source verification; the scalar standardized-residual example is not the complete published test.

## Final review session

Have Anish deliver the five-minute explanation and draft from Lesson 11. Challenge the physical target, approximation, baseline, statistical decision, resource justification, and failure plan one at a time. Ask for a revision before supplying a replacement paragraph. Preserve disagreements and uncertainties that evidence has not settled.
