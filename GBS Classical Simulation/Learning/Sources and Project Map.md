## What these notes establish

The curriculum was written after reading the active specification, original project description, skeptical audit, application draft, and status note, and inspecting the central papers. It does not independently rerun the preliminary numerical results or certify artifact completeness. Reported prior-study figures are attributed to the local project record. Elementary probability, covariance, clipping, and storage examples are instructional calculations.

Grant rules, form fields, deadline, exact code compatibility, and availability of the calibrated target must be checked when acting on them. No deadline or guaranteed allocation is asserted here.

## Reading worksheet

For each paper or project note, record:

1. Its task, physical model, and detector type.
2. Its claimed contribution and the exact passage supporting it.
3. Which results were measured, estimated, predicted, or deferred.
4. Which artifacts would be needed for reproduction.
5. Which metric definitions and assumptions you still need to understand.
6. One sentence you can safely use in the proposal, with its source and boundary.

Bring this worksheet to Lesson 11 rather than relying on remembered headlines.
